1 1st Formula: Weekly Standard
Daily Salary = (Monthly Salary × 12) / (52 × Workdays per Week)

12 months/yr, 52 weeks/yr. Workdays: Mon-Fri = 5, Mon-Half Sat = 5.5, Mon-Sat = 6.

Example: $1000, 5.5 workdays => 12000 / 286 = $41.96 / day
2 2nd Formula: 30-Day Adjusted
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / [30 - (Days off per week × 4)]

Days off: Mon-Fri = 2, Mon-Half Sat = 1.5, Mon-Sat = 1.

Example: $1000, 1.5 days off => 1000 / (30 - 6) = $41.67 / day
3 3rd Formula: Calendar Days Minus Days Off
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / [Days in Month - (Days off per week × 4)]

Uses actual calendar days in month (28–31) minus days off.

Example: $1000, 31 days, 1.5 days off => 1000 / 25 = $40.00 / day
4 4th Formula: Calendar Month (No Holiday)
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / Days in the month

All days in the month, including days off are considered paid.

Example: $1000, 31 calendar days => 1000 / 31 = $32.26 / day
5 5th Formula: Fixed 30 Days (No Holiday)
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 30

Standard commercial 30-day month convention.

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 30 = $33.33 / day
6 6th Formula: Fixed 26 Workdays
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 26

Standard 6-day work week (Monday through Saturday).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 26 = $38.46 / day
7 7th Formula: Fixed 27 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 27

Fixed divisor of 27 workdays.

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 27 = $37.04 / day
8 8th Formula: Fixed 24 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 24

Fixed divisor of 24 workdays (4 weeks × 6 days).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 24 = $41.67 / day
9 9th Formula: Fixed 28 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 28

Fixed divisor of 28 days (4 standard weeks).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 28 = $35.71 / day
10 10th Formula: Fixed 20 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 20

Fixed divisor of 20 workdays (4 weeks × 5 days).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 20 = $50.00 / day
11 11th Formula: Fixed 25 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 25

Fixed divisor of 25 workdays.

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 25 = $40.00 / day
12 12th Formula: Fixed 22 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 22

Standard 5-day work week (Monday through Friday).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 22 = $45.45 / day
13 13th Formula: Fixed 29 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 29

Fixed divisor of 29 days.

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 29 = $34.48 / day
14 14th Formula: Workdays in Month
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / Workdays in the month

Actual working days count in the specific payroll month.

Example: $1000, 21 workdays => 1000 / 21 = $47.62 / day
15 15th Formula: Fixed 15 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 15

Semi-monthly basis (half month wage rate).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 15 = $66.67 / day
16 16th Formula: Fixed 22.5 Days
Daily Salary = Monthly Salary / 22.5

Standard 5.5-day work week (Mon-Fri + Saturday morning).

Example: $1000 => 1000 / 22.5 = $44.44 / day
